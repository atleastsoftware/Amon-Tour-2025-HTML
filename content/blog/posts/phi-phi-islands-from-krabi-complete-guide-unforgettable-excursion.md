# Phi Phi Islands from Krabi: Your Guide to a Perfect Excursion

The Phi Phi Islands, located 45 minutes by speedboat from Krabi, are among Thailand's most famous destinations. This archipelago, made famous by the movie "The Beach," offers crystal-clear turquoise waters, spectacular cliffs, and unforgettable experiences.

## The Phi Phi Archipelago: 6 Islands to Discover

### 1. Phi Phi Don - The Main Island
**Characteristics:**
- The only inhabited island
- Base for accommodations and restaurants
- Tonsai Bay and Loh Dalum Bay
- Lively atmosphere with bars and shops

**Main activities:**
- Viewpoint hike (30 minutes)
- Beach bars and restaurants
- Water sports equipment rental
- Snorkeling in shallow waters
- Traditional massages on the beach

### 2. Phi Phi Leh - The Wild Island
**Famous for:**
- **Maya Bay**: The legendary beach from "The Beach"
- **Viking Cave**: Ancient cave paintings
- **Pileh Lagoon**: Emerald lagoon surrounded by cliffs
- **Loh Samah Bay**: Protected snorkeling spot

**Important:** Maya Bay access is regulated to protect the environment

### 3. Bamboo Island (Koh Phai)
**Paradise for:**
- 360° white sand beach
- Crystal-clear shallow waters
- Perfect snorkeling for beginners
- Picnic in pristine nature

### 4. Mosquito Island (Koh Yung)
**Ideal for:**
- Advanced snorkeling and diving
- Spectacular coral reefs
- Tropical fish in abundance
- Underwater photography

### 5. Bida Nok and Bida Nai
**Diving paradise:**
- Spectacular diving sites
- Sharks and rays
- Advanced level diving
- Experienced divers only

## How to Get to Phi Phi Islands from Krabi

### Speedboat Tours (Recommended)
**Departure:** Ao Nang Pier or Klong Muang
**Duration:** 45 minutes - 1h15
**Price:** 1,800-2,500 THB per person
**Advantages:**
- More islands visited
- Professional guide
- Snorkeling equipment included
- Lunch and refreshments

### Ferry (Budget Option)
**Departure:** Klong Jilad Pier (Krabi Town)
**Duration:** 2h30
**Price:** 450-600 THB per person
**Schedule:** 2-3 departures daily
**Accommodation:** Overnight stay on Phi Phi Don

### Private Speedboat
**Price:** 8,000-15,000 THB (6-10 people)
**Advantages:**
- Flexible schedule
- Choose your itinerary
- Avoid crowds
- Luxury experience

## Best Snorkeling and Diving Spots

### Beginner Level
**Bamboo Island**
- Shallow water (2-5m)
- Excellent visibility
- Diverse tropical fish
- Safe and protected

**Loh Dalum Bay (Phi Phi Don)**
- Easy access from beach
- Calm waters
- Perfect for first experience
- Equipment rental on site

### Intermediate Level
**Pileh Lagoon**
- Enclosed emerald lagoon
- Spectacular cliffs
- Tropical fish
- Unique photos

**Loh Samah Bay**
- Protected from waves
- Rich marine life
- Ideal depth (5-10m)
- Diverse corals

### Advanced Level
**Bida Nok**
- 15-30m depth
- Leopard sharks possible
- Spectacular corals
- Strong currents

**Bida Nai**
- Technical diving
- Whale sharks (rare)
- Advanced certification required
- Professional guide mandatory

## Best Time to Visit

### High Season (November-March)
✅ **Advantages:**
- Perfect weather conditions
- Calm seas
- Optimal visibility for diving
- Less rain

❌ **Disadvantages:**
- More tourists
- Higher prices
- Need to book in advance

### Low Season (May-October)
✅ **Advantages:**
- Fewer tourists
- Attractive prices
- More authentic experience
- Better photo opportunities

❌ **Disadvantages:**
- Risk of rain and storms
- Rougher seas
- Some tours may be cancelled

### Optimal Period: **December to February**

## Practical Tips for a Successful Day

### Health and Safety
- Take seasickness tablets 1 hour before departure
- Apply sunscreen every 2 hours
- Stay hydrated (drink water regularly)
- Respect marine life (no touching)
- Follow guide's safety instructions

### Photography
- Best light: morning (8-10am) and late afternoon (4-6pm)
- Avoid noon for portraits (harsh shadows)
- Use polarizing filter for water
- Respect photography rules in protected areas

### Environmental Respect
- Do not feed fish
- Do not collect shells or corals
- Use reef-safe sunscreen
- Leave only footprints
- Respect closure periods
