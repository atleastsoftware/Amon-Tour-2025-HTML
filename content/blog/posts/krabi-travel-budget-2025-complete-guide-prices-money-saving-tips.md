## Money-Saving Tips and Strategies

### Accommodation Savings
**Book in advance:** 15-30% savings during high season
**Stay in Krabi Town:** 40-50% cheaper than Ao Nang
**Choose guesthouses:** Family-run places often better value
**Long-term rates:** Weekly/monthly discounts available
**Shoulder season:** April-May, September-October for deals

### Food Budget Optimization
**Eat like locals:** Street food and local restaurants
**Avoid tourist areas:** Prices 2-3x higher near beaches
**Happy hours:** Many bars offer drink specials
**Local markets:** Fresh fruit and snacks much cheaper
**Cook occasionally:** Some accommodations have kitchen facilities

### Transportation Savings
**Use songthaews:** Shared taxis are very economical
**Rent motorbike:** Especially for multiple days
**Group tours:** Cheaper per person than private
**Walk when possible:** Many areas are walkable
**Book direct:** Avoid agency commissions

### Activity Budget Tips
**Book local:** Avoid hotel concierges (high markup)
**Compare prices:** Shop around at different agencies
**Group discounts:** Form groups with other travelers
**Free activities:** Beaches, temples, hiking are free
**Happy hour tours:** Some offer afternoon discounts

## Currency and Payment Guide

### Thai Baht (THB) Exchange Rates 2025
**USD:** 1 USD = 32-36 THB
**EUR:** 1 EUR = 35-39 THB
**GBP:** 1 GBP = 40-44 THB
**AUD:** 1 AUD = 22-26 THB

*(Rates fluctuate, check current rates before travel)*

### Best Exchange Options
**Super Rich (orange booths):** Best rates, found in tourist areas
**Bank exchange counters:** Good rates, widely available
**ATMs:** Convenient but 220 THB fee per transaction
**Airport exchange:** Convenient but higher rates
**Hotels:** Avoid - poor exchange rates

### Payment Methods
**Cash preferred:** Many places only accept cash
**Credit cards:** Accepted in hotels, upscale restaurants
**ATM cards:** Widely accepted, notify bank before travel
**Mobile payment:** Limited, mainly in modern establishments

## Seasonal Price Variations

### High Season (December-February)
**Accommodation:** +30-50% premium
**Tours:** Standard prices, advance booking required
**Flights:** Highest prices of the year
**Restaurants:** Tourist area prices peak

### Shoulder Season (March-April, October-November)
**Accommodation:** Moderate pricing
**Tours:** Good availability and pricing
**Weather:** Still good conditions
**Best value:** October-November especially

### Low Season (May-September)
**Accommodation:** 40-60% discounts possible
**Tours:** Lower prices but weather dependent
**Fewer tourists:** More authentic experience
**Rain factor:** Afternoon showers common

## Hidden Costs to Budget For

### Unexpected Expenses
**National park fees:** 200-400 THB per person per park
**Equipment rental:** Snorkel gear, waterproof bags
**Tips:** 10% restaurants, 100-200 THB tour guides
**Laundry:** 40-60 THB per kg
**Travel insurance:** Essential coverage

### Tourist Trap Surcharges
**Hotel tours:** 30-50% markup over local agencies
**Beach restaurant:** 2-3x local restaurant prices
**Taxi vs songthaew:** 5-10x price difference
**Tourist vs local areas:** Significant price gaps

## Emergency Budget Tips

### If You're Running Short
**Switch to hostels:** Immediate accommodation savings
**Eat street food only:** Cut food costs by 70%
**Use public transport:** Songthaews and buses only
**Free activities:** Beaches, temples, hiking
**Happy hours:** Cheaper drinks and food specials

### Extend Your Money
**Move to Krabi Town:** Much cheaper than Ao Nang
**Cook meals:** Buy groceries at local markets  
**Group with travelers:** Share taxi costs and tours
**Negotiate:** Especially for multiple days/services
**Avoid impulse purchases:** Stick to essential spending
