Top 5 Private Catamaran Routes in Thailand: Discover Hidden Islands from Krabi

If you dream of sailing across turquoise waters, discovering untouched islands, and sleeping under a blanket of stars, Thailand’s Andaman Sea is calling.
From Krabi to the Phi Phi Islands, the region offers some of the most stunning seascapes in the world — and the best way to explore them is aboard your private catamaran charter.

At Amon Tour, we operate private cruises aboard Amuitz, a fully refurbished Lagoon 470 catamaran, with an experienced local crew and French-speaking guide. Here are our top 5 routes for an unforgettable adventure at sea.

1. The Hong Islands & Phang Nga Bay — A Paradise of Limestone Peaks

Just north of Krabi, Phang Nga Bay is world-famous for its dramatic cliffs rising straight from emerald waters.
Sail through hidden lagoons, kayak into caves, and drop anchor near Koh Hong, a secluded island with white sand and calm waters.
This route is ideal for a one-day charter, offering the perfect mix of relaxation and discovery.

Highlights: Koh Hong Lagoon, James Bond Island, Koh Roi, and Koh Kudu.
Best for: Families and first-time sailors.

2. Krabi to Koh Phi Phi — Iconic Islands and Sunset Bliss

Set sail southward from Krabi to reach the world-renowned Phi Phi Islands.
Explore Monkey Beach, Bamboo Island, and the legendary Maya Bay (featured in The Beach).
Spend the night in a quiet cove, far from the crowds, and wake up to crystal-clear waters ideal for snorkeling.

Highlights: Sunset at Maya Bay, snorkeling with reef fish, stargazing from the deck.
Best for: Couples or small groups seeking a 2- or 3-day cruise.


3. Koh Rok and Koh Mook — Coral Reefs and Secret Lagoons

Further south lies one of Thailand’s best-kept secrets: Koh Rok Nok and Koh Mook.
These twin islands are part of a protected marine park, home to vibrant coral gardens and turtles.
At Koh Mook, paddle through the famous Emerald Cave, a hidden lagoon accessible only by sea tunnel.

Highlights: Emerald Cave, snorkeling with turtles, pristine beaches.
Best for: Adventurers and nature lovers (3–5-day cruises).

🪸 4. The Similan Islands — Diving Heaven of the Andaman

For experienced sailors or diving enthusiasts, the Similan Islands are a dream come true.
Located northwest of Phuket, this archipelago is ranked among the world’s top 10 dive sites.
Crystal visibility, manta rays, and colorful corals make it an underwater paradise.

Highlights: Diving, crystal visibility, untouched nature.
Best for: Certified divers and 7-day expeditions.

5. Koh Lipe — Thailand’s Maldives

At the southern tip of the Andaman lies Koh Lipe, famous for its turquoise lagoon and powder-white beaches.
Accessible only by boat, it’s the perfect destination for those who want to truly disconnect.
With a private catamaran, the journey itself becomes the destination — every island along the way offers something magical.

Highlights: Long ocean crossings, remote anchorages, tropical serenity.
Best for: Long charters (7 days +), honeymooners, and adventurers.
