# Complete Guide to Krabi 2025: Your Dream Destination in Thailand

## Why Choose Krabi for Your Holiday?

Krabi, located on the west coast of Thailand, is a dream destination that combines white sand beaches, spectacular limestone formations, and unforgettable adventures. This southern Thailand province attracts millions of visitors from around the world each year.

### Krabi Must-Sees

**Railay Beach**: Accessible only by boat, this iconic beach offers impressive limestone cliffs and crystal-clear waters perfect for swimming and rock climbing.

**Phi Phi Islands**: Made famous by the movie "The Beach," these islands offer breathtaking landscapes and exceptional marine life for diving.

**Ao Nang**: The nerve center of Krabi, ideal starting point for exploring the surrounding islands and enjoying seaside restaurants and bars.

### When to Visit Krabi?

The best time to visit Krabi is from November to April, during the dry season. Temperatures are pleasant (25-32°C) and rainfall is minimal.

**High season (December - February)**: Perfect weather but more crowded and high prices
**Mid-season (March - May, October - November)**: Excellent compromise between weather and crowds
**Rainy season (June - September)**: Attractive prices but frequent rains

### Budget and Practical Tips

**Average daily budget for 2 people:**
- Budget: 1,500 - 2,500 THB
- Comfortable: 3,000 - 5,000 THB  
- Luxury: 8,000+ THB

**Transportation:**
- Songthaew (local transport): 50-100 THB
- Tuk-tuk: 150-300 THB
- Scooter rental: 200-300 THB/day
- Taxi: 400-800 THB depending on distance
