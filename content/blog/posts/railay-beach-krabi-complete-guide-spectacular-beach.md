# Railay Beach Krabi: Paradise Accessible Only by Boat

Railay Beach is one of Thailand's most iconic destinations, a peninsula accessible only by boat, surrounded by imposing limestone cliffs and white sand beaches.

## The 4 Beaches of Railay: Detailed Guide

### 1. West Railay - The Main Beach
**Why it's exceptional:**
- 600m of pristine white sand
- Turquoise waters perfect for swimming
- Spectacular sunsets
- Beachside restaurants and bars

**Main activities:**
- Swimming in crystal-clear waters
- Sunbathing on fine sand
- Sunset cocktails
- Traditional beach massages
- Landscape photography

**Atmosphere:** Relaxed during the day, lively in the evening with live music

### 2. East Railay - The Arrival Port
**Characteristics:**
- Arrival area for boats from Ao Nang/Krabi
- Mangroves and rock formations
- Less suitable for swimming (shallow water at low tide)
- Starting point to other beaches (10 min walk)

**Available services:**
- Main pier
- Shops and minimarkets
- Equipment rental (kayak, snorkeling)
- Hotel transfers

### 3. Phra Nang Cave Beach - The Mystical Beach
**Local legend:** Sacred cave of Princess Phra Nang
**Unique highlights:**
- Cave with shrine and traditional offerings
- Beach protected by spectacular cliffs
- World-renowned rock climbing spot
- Impressive limestone formations

**Not to be missed:**
- Visit to the sacred cave (respect traditions)
- Watching climbers on the cliffs
- Exploring rock formations
- Photos with cliff views

### 4. Tonsai Beach - Climbers' Paradise
**Atmosphere:** Bohemian and adventurous
**Specifics:**
- Favorite destination for climbers worldwide
- Budget accommodations and backpackers
- Wilder and more natural beach
- Access on foot from Railay (15 min via jungle trail)

## Rock Climbing at Railay - World Capital

### Why Railay for Climbing?
**Unique formations:**
- Exceptional quality limestone
- Over 700 catalogued climbing routes
- All levels: beginner to expert
- Unique paradisiacal setting worldwide

### Main Climbing Sites

**Phra Nang Beach Wall**
- **Level:** Intermediate to advanced
- **Routes:** 50+ routes, grades 5.6 to 7c+
- **View:** Climbing facing the sea
- **Specialty:** Spectacular overhang climbing

**Thaiwand Wall**
- **Level:** Beginner to intermediate
- **Routes:** Easier routes, ideal for learning
- **Access:** 10 min walk from West Railay
- **School:** Perfect for first lessons

**Ton Sai Tower**
- **Level:** Advanced to expert
- **Specialty:** Multi-pitches and long routes
- **Challenge:** Technical high-level routes
- **View:** Exceptional panorama from the summit

### Recommended Climbing Schools
**Hot Rock Climbing School**
- Internationally certified instructors
- Beginner to advanced courses
- Equipment provided
- Climbing + kayak excursions

**King Climbers**
- Specialized in traditional climbing
- Experienced multilingual guides
- Professional quality equipment
- Safety training

## How to Get to Railay

### From Ao Nang (15 minutes)
**Long-tail boats:**
- Price: 150 THB per person (shared)
- Frequency: Every 30 minutes (8am-6pm)
- Journey: 15-20 minutes depending on weather conditions
- Arrival: East Railay pier

**Private speedboat:**
- Price: 1,500-2,000 THB (6-8 people)
- Advantage: Flexible schedule, West Railay arrival
- Duration: 10 minutes

### From Krabi Town (45 minutes)
**Budget option:** Bus + boat
- Bus Krabi Town → Ao Nang: 60 THB
- Boat Ao Nang → Railay: 150 THB

**Direct option:** Private long-tail
- Price: 2,500-3,000 THB
- Duration: 45 minutes
- Book through hotel or local agency

## Activities and Excursions

### Sea Kayaking
**Cave exploration:**
- Tham Phra Nang Nai (hidden cave)
- Secret lagoon accessible at high tide
- East Railay mangroves
- Unique geological formations

### Snorkeling
**Recommended spots:**
- Reefs near Chicken Island (20 min)
- Poda Island coral garden
- Hong Island (combined tour)

### Hiking and Viewpoints
**Railay Viewpoint**
- Duration: 45 minutes climb
- Difficulty: Moderate (steep trail)
- View: 360° panorama of entire peninsula
- Best time: Late afternoon

## Practical Tips

### Best Time
**Optimal season:** November to March
**Avoid:** May to October (monsoon)
**Climbing:** November to February (perfect conditions)

### Daily Budget (2 people)
- **Budget:** 1,500-2,500 THB
- **Comfortable:** 3,000-5,000 THB
- **Luxury:** 8,000+ THB

### Available Services
✅ **On-site:**
- ATMs (high commission)
- Pharmacy and basic care
- Water sports equipment rental
- Traditional massages
- Laundry

❌ **Unavailable:**
- Cars (boat access only)
- Public transport
- Large supermarkets
- Hospitals (nearest in Krabi Town)
