# The 15 Most Beautiful Beaches in Krabi: Your Guide to Tropical Paradise

Krabi is home to some of the most beautiful beaches in the world. Here is our selection of the 15 must-see beaches for your stay.

## 1. Railay Beach - The Iconic Beach

**Why it's exceptional:** Accessible only by boat, Railay Beach offers a preserved setting with its imposing limestone cliffs.

**Highlights:**
- Immaculate white sand beach
- World-renowned rock climbing
- Spectacular sunsets
- Beachside restaurants and bars

**How to get there:** Boat from Ao Nang (150 THB) or Krabi Town (400 THB)

## 2. Phra Nang Cave Beach - The Mystical Beach

**Local legend:** This beach houses the Phra Nang Princess cave, a sacred place with traditional offerings.

**Recommended activities:**
- Cave exploration
- Snorkeling
- Sea kayaking
- Landscape photography

## 3. Ao Nang Beach - The Nerve Center

**Perfect for:** Families and those who want to be at the heart of the action

**Advantages:**
- Many restaurants and bars
- Easy access to excursions
- Traditional beach massages
- Shopping and nightlife

## 4. Tubkaek Beach - Discreet Luxury

**Ideal for:** Couples seeking romance and tranquility

**Special features:**
- Almost private beach
- Luxury resorts
- View of Hong Islands
- Less crowded

## 5. Klong Muang Beach - Wild Beauty

**Points of interest:**
- Long stretch of fine sand
- Relaxed atmosphere
- Perfect for morning walks
- Authentic seafood restaurants

## 6-10. The Paradise Islands

**Koh Phi Phi Don:** Entertainment and nightlife
**Koh Phi Phi Leh:** Maya Bay and secret lagoons
**Koh Hong:** Protected emerald lagoon  
**Koh Poda:** Postcard beach
**Chicken Island:** Unique rock formation

## 11-15. Hidden Treasures

**Tonsai Beach:** Climbers' paradise
**West Railay:** Magical sunsets
**East Railay:** Mangroves and nature
**Fossil Shell Beach:** Unique geological beach
**Diamond Cave Beach:** Secret beach accessible at low tide

### Tips to Make the Most of It

**Best time:** Early morning or late afternoon
**Protection:** Sunscreen, hat, water
**Respect:** Preserve natural beauty, leave no waste
